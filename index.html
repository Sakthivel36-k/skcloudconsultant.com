What we build

One consultant, the full stack — design, code and cloud.

From a free first website to a full e-commerce store, every build is optimised for speed, search and zero downtime.

Static & Dynamic Websites

Lightweight, fast-loading business and portfolio sites built with modern, low-latency code.

Includes up to 5–7 pages (Home, About, Services, Portfolio, Contact), mobile-responsive layout, contact forms wired to WhatsApp/email, and basic on-page SEO. Built with clean HTML/CSS/JS or React — no bloated page builders slowing you down.

E-commerce Stores

Online shops for small businesses to reach customers directly — built to scale with demand.

Product catalogue, cart & checkout flow, order notifications, and payment gateway integration. Hosted on scalable AWS infrastructure so the store holds up during festive-season traffic spikes.

Cloud Hosting & Maintenance

AWS-based hosting with auto-scaling, backups, and ongoing maintenance — annual AMC available.

Includes S3/CloudFront or EC2 hosting setup, DNS & SSL configuration, automated backups, and an optional Annual Maintenance Contract covering updates, monitoring, and small content changes.

CI/CD Auto-Deploy

Every update ships automatically through a zero-downtime pipeline — no manual uploads, no crashes.

Git-based workflow using Jenkins or GitHub Actions — push code, and it's automatically tested and deployed to production. Reduces manual errors and keeps your site's update history fully tracked.

SEO & Local GEO Tagging

Search-optimised pages with local geo-tags so nearby customers find you first.

Meta titles/descriptions, structured data (schema markup), sitemap & robots.txt setup, and Google Business Profile-friendly geo-tagging so you show up for "near me" searches in your area.

Banners & Photo Animation

Custom digital banners and animated image sections that bring your brand to life.

Web banners, social media creatives, and lightweight CSS/JS-based photo animations for hero sections — no heavy video files slowing down page load.
How we work

From first call to live site, in five steps.

01

Discovery

Understand your business, customers and goals.

A quick call or WhatsApp chat to understand what you sell, who your customers are, and what the website needs to achieve — leads, sales, or just an online presence.
02

Design

Structure the site and design the UI around your brand.

Page structure, layout, and colour/typography choices based on your brand — shared with you for feedback before any code is written.
03

Development

Build and test — lightweight, fast, mobile-first.

The approved design is built with clean, fast-loading code and tested across phone, tablet, and desktop screens before it goes anywhere near a live server.
04

Cloud Deployment

Deploy on AWS/Cloudflare with CI/CD, zero downtime.

The site is deployed to AWS or Cloudflare with an automated pipeline, custom domain connected, and SSL enabled — so it goes live securely on your own domain.
05

Launch & Support

Go live, then ongoing monitoring & maintenance.

Once live, the site is monitored for uptime and performance. Ongoing support and an optional AMC are available for updates, backups, and small changes.
