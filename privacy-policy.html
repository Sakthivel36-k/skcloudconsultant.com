Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

SK Cloud Consultant ("we", "us", "our") operates the website skcloudconsultant.com and provides cloud, web design and hosting consulting services. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use this website or contact us, and how we use it.

1. What Information We Collect

When you fill out our enquiry / quote request form, contact us by email, phone or WhatsApp, we may collect:

  • Your name and business name
  • Phone number / WhatsApp number
  • Email address (when you email us)
  • Project requirement, budget range and any message you share

We do not collect payment details, passwords, or sensitive personal information through this website.

2. How Enquiry Information Is Used

Information submitted through the quote request form is sent directly to our business email (sk.cloudconsultant@gmail.com) so we can respond to your enquiry, prepare a quote, and discuss your project. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Cookies & Analytics

This website may use basic, privacy-respecting analytics (such as page-view counts) to understand how visitors use the site and improve it. We do not use these to build advertising profiles.

4. Third-Party Services

Contacting us via WhatsApp routes through WhatsApp's own platform (Meta), and email routes through your device's email provider (e.g. Gmail). Those services have their own privacy policies which apply to the message once it leaves this site.

5. How We Protect Your Data

Enquiry data received by email is stored in our business email account, protected by standard account security. We retain enquiry information only as long as needed to respond to your request or maintain an ongoing client relationship.

6. Your Rights

You can request that we delete any information you've shared with us, or ask what information we hold about you, by emailing sk.cloudconsultant@gmail.com.
